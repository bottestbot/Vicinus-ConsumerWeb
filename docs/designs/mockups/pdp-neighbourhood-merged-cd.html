Neighbourhood × match — merging C and D

Three ways to fuse Option C (named places and distances around this home) with Option D (your personal match). The shift: the match score stops being a separate card and becomes the lens the neighbourhood evidence is read through — POIs justify the match, and the match explains the POIs. Signed-out visitors see the same layouts with the match elements swapped for area strengths and a "set your priorities" prompt.
