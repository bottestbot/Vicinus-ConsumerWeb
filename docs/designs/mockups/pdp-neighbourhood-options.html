Neighbourhood section — 4 design options

Redesigns of the current "Surrey Context" block. Every data point shown below already exists in GET /neighbourhoods/:slug/detail (livability + breakdown, percentile, market snapshot, POIs with distances, personalization) — no new backend work required for options A–C.

Option A

One livability system

Replace the two generic score cards with the exact livability panel from the neighbourhood page — big score, "Top X% in BC" rank, and the four sub-score bars. The section becomes a preview of the full profile, so "View full profile" feels like zooming in rather than jumping to something unrelated.
